# Mastodon-Post zu Typecase 1.2.0

Bild: `screenshot-light.png` (oder `screenshot-dark.png`) in diesem Ordner

## Englisch (empfohlen, wie die Webseite)

Typecase 1.2 is out, and my calm Markdown writing app for Linux just got a lot more colorful. ✍️

New: images right in the text. And long Markdown for images, links and footnotes folds into small colored pills, so your text stays easy to read.

Also new: an outline, a template assistant, and exports with table of contents, cover page and parts.

Still plain .md files, no account, no network.

https://kleinod.studio/typecase
