Ohne Bild gedacht, deshalb auch ohne Alt-Text. Wer doch ein Bild anhängen möchte: `../typecase-1.2.0/screenshot-light.png` passt, der Alt-Text dazu steht in `../typecase-1.2.0/mastodon-post.md`.

## Englisch (empfohlen, wie die Webseite)

Typecase 1.2.1 is out, a small maintenance update. ✍️

- Narrow windows: the properties pane fits on small screens
- Exports: a missing image's caption appears only once
- German texts in software centers and the app launcher
- Checked against GNOME 51

Typecase doesn't update itself yet, so download the new version and install it over the old one. Your texts and settings stay.

https://kleinod.studio/typecase
