Icons and colors

Give groups and sheets an icon and a color, so they stand out in your library at a glance.

Read the narration

This is Typecase, a calm writing app for Linux. Your library is a folder of plain Markdown files, and at first, every group and sheet looks the same. To make a group stand out, right-click it and choose Icon and Color. Pick one of the nine accent colors from GNOME, then one of twenty-eight icons. Apply, and the group shows them right away. Give the other groups and sheets their own look, too. Characters, places, ideas and open questions now stand out at a glance. The colors follow the light and dark style. And nothing is hidden away: a sheet keeps its icon and color in its front matter, a group in its own folder, so they travel along when you move, copy or sync them.
